Fifteen minutes alone in an empty room. No phone. Nothing to read. Just your thoughts — and a button that gives you an electric shock. Two out of three men pressed it.

What was really going on in that 2014 experiment? We look at mind wandering, our reflex to reach for a screen, and an older question: how do you learn to be alone with yourself?

From a psychology lab to Pascal, from al-Ghazali's retreat to khalwa and dhikr in the Sufi path I follow, the Karkariya. And a simple two-minute exercise to try tonight.
